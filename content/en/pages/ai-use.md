# How we use AI

We want you to know exactly where AI is and isn't involved in PranaDin.

## Where we use AI

- **Drafting text.** Some English text, and the first drafts of our Hindi and Marathi translations, are written with the help of AI tools. Hindi and Marathi text is shown as a draft straight away, and a person on our team is reviewing it.
- **Checking facts.** We use AI tools to help search for published sources. Every festival date is still confirmed against two named sources, which you can see in the app.

## Where we don't use AI

- **No AI voices.** PranaDin has no recorded or synthetic voice at all. Guidance is written text, with soft chimes.
- **No AI images.** We use no AI-generated pictures.
- **No synthetic chanting.** If we add mantras in future, they will be chanted by real people and credited by name.
- **No AI chat.** There is no chatbot giving spiritual or health advice.
- **No AI in your journal.** Your journal stays on your phone. No one, and no AI, reads it.

The panchang (tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and sunset) is worked out by ordinary astronomical calculation on your phone, not by AI.

If anything here changes, we will update this page and label any AI-made material where it appears.
