# Sources and credits

## Breathing and meditation

All practice text is written by the PranaDin content team in our own words. Hindi and Marathi versions are checked by a member of our team before they appear.

- **Nadi Shodhana (alternate-nostril breathing)** and **Bhramari (humming-bee breath)**: technique and cautions follow the *Common Yoga Protocol*, Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India ([PDF](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/pdf/common-yoga-protocol-english.pdf)). Our beginner versions use equal breaths and no breath-holding.
- **Sama Vritti (equal breathing)** and **Deergha Pranayama (three-part breath)**: widely taught techniques, described in our own words. No breath-holding.
- **Breath awareness** and **Body scan** meditations: our own scripts. The so'ham phrase is traditional Sanskrit.

## Festival and observance dates

Each date is checked against two published sources. You can see the sources on each festival in the Calendar. The sources we used most:

- Drik Panchang, Hindu calendars 2026 and 2027 (New Delhi) — [drikpanchang.com](https://www.drikpanchang.com/)
- Government of India, Department of Personnel and Training: lists of holidays for 2026 and 2027 for Central Government offices in Delhi
- Hindu Blog festival calendars 2026 and 2027 — [hindu-blog.com](https://www.hindu-blog.com/)
- timeanddate.com, holidays in India — [timeanddate.com](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/india/)
- For Christian, Jewish, Buddhist and Islamic dates: EWTN, Catholic Church Times, Aish.com, CNN, Lion's Roar, Wego and others, named on each item

Dates checked on 14 November 2026. Islamic dates depend on the sighting of the moon.
