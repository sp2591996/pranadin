# Corrections

We check our content carefully, but we will sometimes get things wrong: a date, a translation, a description of a tradition. When that happens, we want to fix it quickly and say so openly.

## How to report a mistake

Tap **Report a mistake** on any festival, or use [Send feedback]({{feedbackLink}}) in Me. Tell us what is wrong and, if you can, where the correct information comes from. Please don't include private details.

## What we do

1. **Within 24 hours:** we acknowledge reports where you've left an email address.
2. **Within 72 hours:** we fix the item or remove it while we check.
3. **We record it here:** what changed, and when.

If a festival date is in doubt, we hide it rather than show a guess. If a Hindi or Marathi text reads wrong, tell us and we will correct it.
