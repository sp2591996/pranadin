# Health and content disclaimer

## 1. Not medical advice

The practices in PranaDin (breathing, meditation and journaling prompts) are for general wellbeing and education only. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and they are not a substitute for professional medical, psychological or psychiatric care. Do not stop or change any treatment because of something in the app. We make no claim that any practice will cure a condition.

## 2. Practise safely

- Practise on an empty or light stomach, sitting or lying on a stable surface, within a range that feels comfortable.
- **Stop at once** if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness, chest discomfort, breathlessness or panic. Breathe normally and rest until it passes. If it doesn't pass, get medical help.
- **Breathing practices.** The four practices in PranaDin are gentle and never ask you to hold your breath. Even so, check with a doctor first if you are pregnant, or have heart disease, high or low blood pressure, glaucoma, epilepsy, a hernia, a recent surgery or injury, or a lung condition. Never practise breathing exercises while driving or in water.
- **Meditation** can sometimes bring up difficult feelings or memories. You can open your eyes, move or stop at any time. If you have a history of trauma, psychosis or severe anxiety, practise with a qualified professional.
- Beginners, older adults and anyone with a health condition may find it helpful to learn from a qualified teacher.

## 3. In an emergency

Call **112** for emergency services anywhere in India. For mental-health support, call **Tele-MANAS on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416**, the Government of India's toll-free helpline. Outside India, call your local emergency number.

## 4. Your responsibility

You choose whether and how to practise. By using the practices, you accept responsibility for checking that they suit you.

## 5. Religious and cultural content

Festivals, sacred days and traditional terms are shared respectfully, for learning and personal practice. PranaDin does not claim religious authority, does not rank or judge any faith, and does not promise spiritual, material, health or astrological results from any practice or date. Traditions, interpretations and dates differ by region, community and lineage, so please follow your own tradition and teacher. If you find anything inaccurate or disrespectful, please [tell us]({{feedbackLink}}) and we will review it promptly.

## 6. Panchang and festival dates

Tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and sunset are calculated on your phone for the city you choose. The tithi shown is the one prevailing at local sunrise. Results are indicative and may differ from your local temple or printed panchang. Festival dates are checked against two published sources each, but regional and community calendars can differ by a day or more, and Islamic dates depend on the sighting of the moon.

## 7. AI and this content

Some of our text is drafted with the help of AI tools and then checked by a person before it is published. PranaDin uses no AI-generated voices or images. Read more in [How we use AI](ai-use).
