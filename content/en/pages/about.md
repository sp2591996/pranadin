# About PranaDin

PranaDin (प्राणदिन) means "daily breath". It gives you a short practice each day, sized to the time you have: a few minutes of breathing, a few minutes of stillness and one line of reflection. Alongside it, a calendar shows the festivals and sacred days of many traditions.

## What PranaDin is

- **A daily practice.** Four gentle breathing techniques, two meditation guides and a private journal. None of the breathing practices ask you to hold your breath.
- **A calendar of many traditions.** Hindu, Sikh, Jain, Buddhist, Christian, Islamic, Jewish and national days, each described in its own terms. We don't rank or compare faiths.
- **Private by design.** There is no account. Your practice history and journal stay on your phone.
- **In your language.** English, Hindi and Marathi. Hindi and Marathi text is a draft translation, shown straight away while a person reviews it. If something reads wrong, please tell us.

## What PranaDin is not

- It is not medical advice or treatment. Please read our [health disclaimer](disclaimer).
- It is not a religious authority. Follow your own tradition and teacher where they differ from what you see here.
- We don't publish horoscopes, predictions, remedies or "auspicious timings", and we don't sell pujas.

## How we choose what to publish

Every festival date is checked against two published sources before it appears. Every breathing practice uses our own wording and follows published guidance, such as the Ministry of AYUSH Common Yoga Protocol. You can see each source on our [Sources and credits](sources) page.

## This is a beta

PranaDin is free during the beta and has no ads. Some things will change, and we will make mistakes. If you spot one, please [tell us]({{feedbackLink}}). See how we handle [corrections](corrections).
