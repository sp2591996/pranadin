### 1. Not medical advice
Everything in PranaDin (breathing practices, meditation, journaling prompts, and information about traditions) is general information for personal wellbeing. It isn't meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any illness. It doesn't replace care from a doctor, psychologist or psychiatrist. **Don't stop or change any treatment because of something in this app.** We make no claim that any practice will improve any health condition.

### 2. Practise safely
- Sit comfortably on a stable chair or floor, ideally on an empty or light stomach. Breathe within your comfortable range. Never strain.
- **Stop at once** if you feel pain, dizziness, light-headedness, tingling, chest discomfort, breathlessness or panic. Breathe normally and rest. If symptoms don't settle, seek medical help.
- **Never practise breathing exercises while driving, operating machinery, in water, or when you need to stay alert.**
- PranaDin's breathing practices are gentle and **don't include breath-holding or forceful breathing.** If you've been told to avoid breathing exercises, or you are pregnant, or have a heart, lung or blood-pressure condition, epilepsy, glaucoma, or recent surgery or injury, **ask your doctor before trying them.**
- **Meditation and reflection** can sometimes bring up difficult feelings or memories. That is not a failure. It's fine to open your eyes, move, or stop. If you live with trauma, psychosis, severe anxiety or depression, please practise with the support of a qualified professional.
- If you are thinking of harming yourself, please call **Tele-MANAS 14416** or **112** now, or go to the nearest hospital.
- Beginners, older adults and anyone with a health condition may benefit from learning with a qualified teacher.

### 3. Your choice and responsibility
You decide whether and how to practise. By using the practices, you accept responsibility for checking that they suit you.

### 4. Religious and cultural content is for information
Festivals, observances, verses, panchang terms and traditions are presented respectfully, for learning and personal practice. PranaDin:
- doesn't claim religious authority, and doesn't rank or judge any faith;
- doesn't promise any spiritual, material, health or astrological result from any practice, verse or date;
- doesn't offer predictions, remedies, Rahu Kaal, muhurta or paid pujas.

Traditions, meanings and dates differ by region, sampradaya and community. Please follow your own tradition and teacher. If anything seems inaccurate or disrespectful, tell us at **⟦contact email⟧** and we'll review it promptly.

### 5. Panchang and festival dates
Panchang timings (tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and sunset) are **indicative**. They're calculated for the city you choose and may differ from your local temple or printed panchang. Festival dates come from a curated list, each checked against two published sources. Islamic dates may vary by moon sighting. Please confirm important dates within your tradition.

Text in PranaDin was drafted with the help of AI tools and **reviewed by a person** before it appears. We don't use AI-generated voices or images. Mistakes can still happen, and we correct them on the Corrections page.
