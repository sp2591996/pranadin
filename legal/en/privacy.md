## 1. The short version
- You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, phone number or email to use PranaDin.
- Your **journal, mood, practice history, streak and settings stay on your phone.** We can't see them, and we can't recover them.
- **Anonymous usage statistics are off unless you turn them on.** You can turn them off anytime.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers or cookies for tracking.

## 2. Who we are
PranaDin is a free beta app run by Sachin, an individual based in Mumbai, India ("we", "us"). There is no company yet. For the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are responsible (the "Data Fiduciary") for the small amount of personal data described below.
Contact for anything in this notice: **sachin@gmail.com**.

## 3. What stays only on your phone
These are saved in your browser's storage on your device. **They are never sent to us or anyone else:**
- journal entries and mood choices;
- practices completed, minutes, streak and week dots;
- your language, goal, practice length, city and text-size settings;
- whether you've seen the safety notes.

**Please note:**
- Anyone who uses your phone and opens PranaDin in the same browser can read your journal. There's no lock in the beta.
- If you clear your browser data, uninstall the browser or lose your phone, this data is gone. Use **Me → Export journal** to keep a copy.
- **Me → Clear my data** deletes all of it from your phone at once.

## 5. Why we're allowed to process it
- **Anonymous usage events, error reports and feedback:** your consent, which you give by switching usage sharing on or by submitting feedback. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it (Me → Usage sharing → Off). Data sent before you withdrew may stay with the provider until its retention period ends.
- **Technical request data:** needed to deliver the website you asked for, and kept for security.

## 6. Service providers and transfers outside India
We use one free service provider that processes data for us: **Vercel Inc.** (hosting). We don't use analytics or error-reporting tools in the beta, and feedback is only by email to sachin@gmail.com. They may store data outside India (for example in the USA or the EU). Indian law currently allows this, except to countries the Government restricts. We don't sell or share personal data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## 7. Your rights
You can, free of charge:
- **withdraw consent** (switch usage sharing off; ask us to delete your feedback);
- **ask** what personal data we hold about you, and **correct** or **erase** it;
- **nominate** someone to exercise your rights if you die or can't act;
- **complain** to us, and if you're not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Because we don't know who you are, we can usually find your data only if you give us something to look for it by (for example, the email you used in feedback). Data that stays on your phone is fully in your control: use Export or Clear my data.
Write to **sachin@gmail.com**. We'll reply within 7 days and aim to finish within 30 days.

## 8. Security
The app is served only over HTTPS, uses strict browser security settings, and doesn't load scripts or fonts from other websites. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects personal data we hold, we'll inform affected people (where we can reach them) and the authorities, as the law requires.

## 9. Children
PranaDin's beta is meant for people aged **18 and over**. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us data (for example, through the feedback form), write to us and we'll delete it.
